Published Crosswords

My specialty is making wide-open themeless crosswords, which are the most difficult puzzles that newspapers offer. This is my favourite type of crossword to create, since the grids turn out so beautiful. I can't reproduce the clues for licensing reasons, but I can show you the grids. Enjoy!
