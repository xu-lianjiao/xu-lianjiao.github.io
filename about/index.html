About Me

Hey there! I'm Julian (he/him), and I'll be entering my final year of an Integrated Master's in Maths and CS at the University of Oxford.

I grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area, surrounded by STEM. Moving to Oxford allowed me to study my two favourite subjects full-time at a high level of rigour. My academic focus is in applications of probability and machine learning. My upcoming Master's research project is on adaptive clinical trials, where accumulating evidence is modeled as a betting martingale. This will allow researchers to peek at data and choose to stop early, while still preserving statistical validity. I hope you enjoy looking through this site and learn who I really am!
